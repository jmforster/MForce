## 1. Oboe "trading licks" — mechanism found, two patches staged (REVIEW 76)

Tool: tools/oboe_licks.py (phrase stamper matching the transport's auto
rule, CLI render harness, fundamental-collapse classifier fmt =
E[1–3.5k]/E[0.2–1k], >50 = collapsed).

**The mechanism.** The wind loop has two stable ignition states:
fundamental-carried (Matt's "nasal oboe target") and H2-carried,
fundamental collapsed, ~8 dB louder (his "mellow flute/clarinet" — the
ear reads the near-pure strong H2 as a flute an octave up). A phrased
line inherits its breath note's ignition outcome through the holds —
phrasing exposed the instability, it didn't create it. Measured basins
(8 seeds, v0.8, ~1 s): D6 flips 3/8, E6 1/8, F#6 1/8, G6 0, A6 0;
velocity 0.7 and 0.9 both all-fundamental (narrow window); warm-tube
residue and 12 s gaps measured irrelevant (phrase 2 tips from virgin
silence). Deterministic: identical md5 across repeat renders. In his
WAV (correct grid: full psg at 120 bpm — I first gridded it at 2× the
tempo and mislabeled the per-note table; caught the same day via his
side-chat note; the corrected table is unambiguous): phrases 1/3 =
fundamental state, uniform; phrases 2/4 = H2 state, uniform; 1 vs 3
byte-close repeats. His two D-starting lines flip because D6 is the
coin-flip pitch.

**Why re-anchor couldn't fix it**: the breath (first) note draws from
the free-running stream position; the character is set at ignition,
before per-note pinning matters. (His embedded question — re-anchor
doesn't touch the driven tube — confirmed correct.)

**Levers measured:**
- Junction middle slope ×0.7/×0.85 → fundamental state UNANIMOUS
  (0 tips/16); ×1.15 → more H2 flips (3–4/8); ×1.3 non-monotonic.
  No slope makes the H2 state dominant.
- Middle-slope asymmetry (positive side only): moves odds, never past
  ~half. Attack-time (Drive_env stage 0 percent 0.1–0.7): inert.
- Deterministic H2 igniter ping (15 ms sine at 2f into the loop
  input): forces H2 state 8/8 at every level ≥0.02 on ~1 s notes, but
  FAILS on half-note phrase starts (the percent-mode drive attack
  stretches to ~800 ms and the ping dies before ignition — backlog
  72's duration-marginality in one sentence).
- Continuous H2 bias (same sine, −46 dB, whole note): H2 state
  unanimous — 8/8 seeds × all velocities 0.6–0.9 × both durations,
  28/28 line notes both tempos.

**Staged (renders/dsp/audition/oboe_nasal1/):** oboe_steady.json

## 2. Trombone transitions — diagnosed, not built (74 fold)

Controlled two-note renders (scratch), F#5→G5 and E5→A4, glide 0/5/15/
60 ms, slur vs tongue. THE MECHANISM: during a glide the bore spends
50–150 ms between partials — the oscillation collapses (−38 dB dip at
glide 15), then the new note re-ignites with a ~+2% overshoot (the
per-attack squeak; same signature as 74a7, whose ceilings only covered
fresh attacks) while the old partial's residue rises under it (the
blend). Blend length = re-ignition time, not lip lag. Matt's knob
findings reproduce: 5 ms better, 60 ms worse, tongue no help (pitch
still glides), glide 0 fastest/cleanest but is the old tick. E5→A4 is
a genuine 140 ms glissando. Fix candidates RECORDED in REVIEW 74, not
built (patch unfinished, his call): tongue+instant-retune on partial
crossings; slur-gated breath kick (patch-only) to shorten re-ignition.
"All breath" transport option SHIPPED (mode 3, = pre-articulation).
