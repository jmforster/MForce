## Front 1 — CMA-ES stage a: patch scorer [metric] ✓ (commit d5c9f2d)

Built the render→score harness the whole ears project hangs on:
- `refmetrics.py` — shared 4-dimension timbre extractors, so reference and
  candidate are measured identically: harmonic envelope (log-amp/harmonic),
  motion stats (derive_motion resid cents / rates / coherences + amp fluct),
  inter-harmonic broadband ratio per band (the bow-noise gap), attack
  (per-band rise + inter-band onset spread).
- `iowa_reference.py` — precomputes the Iowa target JSON (per-note envelope/
  broadband/attack for C3/G3/D4/A4 + motion medians pooled over 8 samples).
  Verifies against the documented Iowa numbers: resid 7.3c@8.3Hz coh~0,
  amp 50%@1.4Hz coh 0.5.
- `score_candidate.py` — renders a patch on the register-spanning scoring
  notes, prints the weighted 4-term breakdown (0.35/0.25/0.2/0.2) plus every
  motion sub-metric so weight tuning stays a Matt's-ears decision.

Stage-a acceptance (from spec): `term2(v5_04_cal)=1.008 < motion-zeroed
control=1.368` → PASS. The scorer also immediately surfaces the real gaps:
term3 broadband ≈ 2.2 (additive has ~no inter-harmonic energy vs Iowa bow
noise), and amp-fluct rate reads the 18 Hz frequency-motion coupling through
the formant slopes rather than the 1.4 Hz shimmer (the run-1 FM→AM finding),
i.e. the measurement is honest about what the render actually does.
