## 1. WO1 — verifying the attack-only claim (74a5)

`gen_trombone4.py attack`, one note per render, 2.5 s long, attack window
5–120 ms, settled window 1.20–2.20 s.

### (a) The claim is half right, and the half that is wrong matters

Matt heard two things and said them in one sentence. Measured separately:

**"the sustain phases sound identical, just louder" — not literally true.**
Attempt 3's settled window does move with dynamics on three of four notes
(+18.5% / +33.8% / +27.0% on A#3 / C5 / F5). F2 is the exception at +0.8%.

**"missing any swell or brightening *after* the attack" — exactly true.**
The centroid track at ff, sampled through one note:

| note | 0.02 s | 0.05 s | 0.10 s | 0.20 s | 0.40 s | 0.80 s | 1.6 s | 2.2 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A#3 | 341 | 435 | 498 | 507 | 505 | 505 | 505 | 505 |
| C5 | 346 | 375 | 521 | 578 | 572 | 572 | 572 | 572 |
| F5 | 418 | 413 | 489 | 509 | 506 | 506 | 506 | 506 |

The note reaches its timbre in about 200 ms and then does not move again —
the same three digits for two full seconds. There is no evolution inside a
held note because there is nothing inside the model that changes during one:
velocity is constant for the note's whole life, so the breath is too. That is
a real gap and it is NOT what this round fixed (§8).

**And the third thing, which neither of us named.** Above the working point
the instrument was nearly dead: A#3's mf → ff was +0.5% and +1.1 dB, because
A#3's own measured ceiling is only 1.135× its mf pressure (45.4 vs 40 kPa).
Matt plays at 0.8 and pushes up from there, so this is most of what he was
hearing.

### (b) Attribution — one sentence per note, with the numbers

First, two measurement traps had to come out, and one of them invalidates an
attempt-3 finding.

**The probe was reading through a clamp.** `gen_trombone3.probe_signal` reads
the probed node through a `{"tap"}`, and a tap resolves to a **guarded**
`RefSource` which clamps every read to ±8 (`core/dsp_value_source.h:164`;
only `{"tap"}` sets `guard=true`, `patch_loader.cpp:105-122`). Measured side
by side on A#3 at ff:

```
Lip    guarded peak    8.000   raw peak   70.029   max|diff| 6.20e+01
Pe     guarded peak    2.925   raw peak    2.925   max|diff| 0.00e+00
Bore   guarded peak    1.474   raw peak    1.474   max|diff| 0.00e+00
```

So attempt 3's *"at ff the lip displacement sits on the engine's ±8 clamp for
53–69% of each cycle"* was the probe's own clamp — it could not have returned
anything else. `probe(..., raw=True)` reads through a plain second `{"ref"}`
instead (an unguarded `RefSource`), and agrees with the tap exactly wherever
the signal is inside ±8, which is the check that it is the same tap.

**The engine's ±8 is not in the signal path here anyway.** The lip is read by
`Qn` through a plain `{"ref"}`, never a tap. What *does* clamp the lip opening
is a knot we wrote ourselves: `flow_nodes()` gives `Qn` three knots spanning
±8, and a CurveNode in points mode holds its end knots (`curve_node.h:12`).

Measured rail fractions in the settled window, attempt 3's configuration
(`gen_trombone4.py attrib` for the engine rails, `lipdom` for the lip, which
counts the **positive** side only — the negative side is the valve being
shut, which is the model working):

| | F2 | A#3 | C5 | F5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lip over **our** Qn knot, pp | 67.9% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| ...at mf | 68.6% | 63.9% | 47.3% | 37.5% |
| ...at ff | 69.1% | 64.5% | 54.5% | 52.6% |
| **engine ±8** on `{tap: Pe}` | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| **engine ±8** on `{tap: Bore}` | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| our AbsDp4 knot (±20) | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
| our steepener knot | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |

The travelling wave at the steepener's input, settled window, is **linear in
the breath, not saturating**:

| note | pp drive rms | ff drive rms | span | pressure ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F2 | 0.2512 | 0.8691 | +10.8 dB | 3.80× (drive 3.46×) |
| A#3 | 0.0458 | 0.6380 | +22.9 dB | 13.8× (drive 13.9×) |
| C5 | 0.0643 | 1.0823 | +24.5 dB | 16.3× (drive 16.8×) |
| F5 | 0.0684 | 1.5627 | +27.2 dB | 21.9× (drive 22.8×) |

And the steepener's transfer **does** differ across those amplitudes —
ablated at depth 0 vs 0.03, settled window only:

| note | Δcentroid at pp | at mf | at ff |
|---|---|---|---|
| F2 | −3 | −4 | −3 |
| A#3 | +5 | +52 | +56 |
| C5 | +7 | +61 | **+125** |

So, the attribution, one sentence per note:

- **F2** — nothing rails in the engine, the loop does not self-limit (drive
  +10.8 dB), and the clamp is not it; its sustain is flat because the
  steepener contributes **nothing at F2 at any dynamic** (−3 Hz at pp and at
  ff), and the +24.9% of brightness its *flow* nonlinearity does produce is
  then removed by the fixed output filter.
- **A#3** — the loop is linear in breath to within 1% and nothing rails; the
  instrument produces **+80.8%** of settled centroid motion and the fixed
  filter delivers **+18.5%** of it; above mf specifically it produces almost
  nothing because the note only has 13% more breath to give.
- **C5** — same shape: **+53.0%** produced, **+33.8%** delivered, no rail
  anywhere, drive linear over 24.5 dB.
- **F5** — the widest: **+163.0%** produced, **+27.0%** delivered, i.e. the
  filter is removing about five sixths of this note's dynamic timbre.

### (c) The clamp lever, measured and rejected

WO2's first lever was to rescale the lip's normalised units so its operating
range fits inside ±8. Measured directly instead (`gen_trombone4.py lipdom`),
by widening `Qn`'s knot at identical slope over 8 → 16 → 32 → 64 → 128 → 256:

| A#3 settled centroid | dom 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 | 128 | 256 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mf | 503 | 507 | 507 | 507 | 507 | 507 |
| ff | 505 | 511 | 511 | 511 | 511 | 511 |

Taking the lip clamp from "holding two thirds of every cycle" to "never
touching it"
moves the settled centroid by **1.2%** and the rms by 0.7%, because the
Bernoulli flow saturates on its own the moment `q² ≫ 4|pm − 2p⁻|` — the flow
tends to `pm − 2p⁻` regardless of how far the valve opens. On F2 widening it
**breaks the tuning**: at pp, three of the six domains tested put the note on
a different partial (−1285 cents). So the clamp is not load-bearing, the
rescale is not the fix, and **no engine round was needed** — 74a4 can be
closed on evidence rather than escalated.

## 2. WO2 — what was actually throwing the brightness away

`gen_trombone4.py voice` probes `Trans4` (the last node before the voicing
lowpass) and the output, same note, same window:

| note | pp → ff BEFORE the filter | AFTER it |
|---|---|---|
| F2 | **+24.9%** | +0.8% |
| A#3 | **+80.8%** | +18.5% |
| C5 | **+53.0%** | +33.8% |
| F5 | **+163.0%** | +27.0% |

Attempt 2's voicing lowpass is two cascaded SVF sections at a **fixed**
1100 Hz — the corner Matt picked himself ("Line beats line_brighter"). He
picked it while the instrument was stuck permanently at forte by attempt 3's
two gain stages, so it was chosen to tame a sound that had no dynamics. A
fixed corner sitting exactly where the energy moves when you blow harder
removes most of the movement, and the table is what that costs.

### The fix: the corner rides the breath

One new node, `Vk`, a CurveNode on `__perf_v` feeding both SVF `cutoffFreq`
pins. Inserted immediately before `Voice0` (a `{"ref"}` may not point
forward), which leaves `Vdn` as `__perf_v`'s advancing consumer so nothing in
the dynamics subgraph moves. Zero engine code.

The two ends are **solved, not picked** (`gen_trombone4.py corner`, coarse
grid then a refine pass). The criterion: the filter may set where the overall
tone sits, but it may not change **how much the instrument brightens between
soft and hard**. So for each note the target output centroid is
`centroid_out(mf) × centroid_pre(v)/centroid_pre(mf)`, and the corner is the
one minimising the log-rms error across F2/A#3/C5/F5.

| velocity | corner | how |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 0.25 | **550 Hz** | solved, log-rms error 0.182 (0.273 at 997 Hz, the nearest point tested to the fixed corner) |
| 0.80 | **1100 Hz** | PINNED — Matt's own pick, untouched |
| 1.00 | **1600 Hz** | solved, log-rms error 0.106 (0.201 at 997 Hz) |

Measured cost of the pp end: pp notes come out **4.5 dB quieter** than attempt
3's pp (rms 0.00212 → 0.00126 on A#3), with identical pitch and identical
periodicity (−2.0 cents, lock 1.00 in both). That is dark-and-quiet by
construction, and it is reported rather than hidden.

0.8·f0, the two detrending methods return different answers on every note

slow in the low octaves" because f0 *is* slow there — below roughly 40 pulses
per second a pulse train stops fusing into a pitch and is heard as separate

the solved range (`gen_trombone4.py below`):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Matt: *"In octave 1 that is \*all\* you hear, irrespective of note hold. In
octave 2 (starting with D2/36.7Hz) you get a sustained note."* Measured: at
C1 and F1 there is **no oscillation at all** — rms 0.0000, lock 0.01–0.03, so

**No fix attempt was made on the rate**, because there is no rate to tune —

## 4. WO4 — the max-velocity squeak (74a7)

`gen_trombone4.py squeak`. A squeak = the loop off the written partial during
the attack, measured as the pitch of each overlapping attack slice (six
periods long, floor 60 ms) against the written note.

| velocity | slices off the written partial, summed over 14 notes midi 53–79 |
|---|---|
| 0.80 | 10 |
| 0.90 | 32 |
| 1.00 | 37 |

It is **not** the clamp (every rail measures 0% in §1b) and it is **not** a
clean overblow: the off-slices read ×1.07–×1.25, i.e. the attack pitch
overshoots and settles rather than grabbing the next partial. One note is a
genuine break — **C#6 at velocity 1.0 drops a full octave** (−1198 cents in
the settled window, 14 of 25 attack slices off).

Fix, per the dispatch's "too much pressure" option: each note's ff pressure is
now walked down (×0.9 a step) until its **attack** is stable, not just its
settled pitch — which is all attempt 3's ceiling measurement ever asked.
`gen_trombone4.py ffstab`, gate = at most 16% of attack slices off, none more
than ×1.35 away, settled pitch within 15 cents, harmonic lock above 0.35.

**6 of 51 notes moved** — D#5, E5, A#5, B5, C6, C#6 — and 45 keep attempt 3's
ff exactly. Velocity 0.8 is untouched either way, since the ff map only acts
above it: the "not-great trumpet player" squeak Matt likes is still there.

Two metric bugs of mine were found and fixed on the way, both of which would
have cut good notes' ff on an artifact:

- a fixed 60 ms slice is 2.6 periods at F2 and its autocorrelation lag search
  runs off the end of the slice — it reported F2 jumping 42% sharp and would
  have taken 5 dB off its ff;
- counting *downward* excursions catches the autocorrelation picking a
  subharmonic (readings of ×0.51, ×0.42, ×0.34 on single slices); with those
  counted, 25 of 51 notes were pulled in, including A#3 all the way down to
  its mf. Only upward excursions count now, and a loop that really drops a
  partial is caught by the settled-pitch test, which is how C#6 is caught.

The 0.8 parity is *not* bit-identity and is not claimed as such: widening the
steepener's knots from ±4 to ±8 at the same slope changes the two knot values,
so the interpolation arithmetic rounds differently in its last bits. The
transfer is the same line; the difference is one quantization step.

`ladder_low` lands −7.4 dB under the reference because its ff peak eats the
headroom and the cell is levelled as one so its internal dynamics survive.

The ignition gate is a pitch-and-periodicity test, not a loudness test, and
this round had to say so louder than attempt 3 did: the darker pp corner
drops pp levels 4.5 dB, so attempt 3's 0.0015 rms floor would have failed 22
notes that lock on the written pitch to the same cent and the same 1.00
periodicity. The floor is now the repo's true-silence floor and the pp level
is reported instead.

## 8. Open, and not in this build

- **Nothing changes inside a held note** (§1a). Velocity is fixed for a
  note's life, so the breath is, so the timbre is — flat to three digits from
  200 ms to 2.2 s. Matt's "swell or brightening *after* the attack" needs a
  breath that moves during a note: a continuous controller (74/39 — his
  keyboard has no wheel or aftertouch), or a per-note breath contour, or
  PerformSource carrying a performance curve. This is a design question, not
  a tweak, and it is the honest remaining half of his verdict.
- **Above mf some notes still have almost no breath left** — A#3's own
  ceiling is 1.135× its mf. That is 74a3/74b territory (the lip/bore pairing),
  not a mapping.
- **74a4 (the engine ±8 clamp) is answered and closes**: it never touched the
  lip in the signal path, the rails measure 0.0% in sustain, and our own Qn
  knot — the one that *does* hold — is worth 1.2% when removed entirely.
- The rattle thread has one attempt left (§3) and no candidate mechanism
  standing; the next move there is Matt's ears on `ladder_low`.
- Everything in attempt 1 §9, attempt 2 §7 and attempt 3 §8 stays true: the
  slide is not a slide, the bore is a lumped loop rather than a waveguide, no
  viscothermal loss, minimum-phase-fitted bell magnitude, non-inverting loop
  sign, and nothing anywhere is fitted to a recording of a real trombone.
