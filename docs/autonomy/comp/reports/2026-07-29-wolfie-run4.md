## Front 3 — #4b higher-order + smoothing variants [metric] ✓

ngram3_backoff and ngram2_addk shipped in the same harness (they share
`NGramModel`). Two-seed bake-off (K=80, L=16):

| method | int_jsd | comp_mean | comp_p10 | big_leap | range_mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| corpus (ceiling) | 0.006 | 0.80 | 0.67 | 0.04 | 12.6 |
| uniform (floor) | 0.51 | 0.30 | 0.23 | 0.75 | 97 |
| unigram (order-0) | 0.014 | 0.77 | 0.65 | 0.045 | 16.8 |
| ngram1 | 0.014 | **0.816** | 0.71 | 0.043 | 14.8 |
| **ngram2_backoff** (shipped) | 0.014 | 0.807 | **0.71** | 0.050 | **14.0** |
| ngram3_backoff | 0.013 | 0.793 | 0.67 | 0.046 | 13.9 |
| ngram2_addk | 0.084 | 0.57 | 0.41 | 0.24 | 38 |

(values averaged over seeds 1234/77; both seeds agree within ~0.01–0.02.)

Findings, stable across seeds:

1. **Context earns its keep mainly through range control, not interval fit.**
   unigram already matches the corpus interval distribution well (int_jsd 0.014)
   — the marginal *is* mostly stepwise. What the marginal can't do is stop the
   walk drifting: range 16.8 vs corpus 12.6. Adding context pulls range in
   (ngram2 14.0, ngram3 13.9). uniform (no structure) is the control: range ~97,
   big_leap 0.75 — a 4-octave random walk, the textbook computer-music tell.

2. **The shipped order-2 backoff sits at the sweet spot.** ngram1 edges it on raw
   composite (0.816 vs 0.807) but with looser range; ngram2 gives the tightest
   range among the high-composite methods at essentially the same composite/p10.
   ngram3 tightens range a hair more but the composite slips (0.79) — more
   backoff on sparse 3-contexts, and it starts leaning on repetition. Net: the
   shipped choice is *validated*, not arbitrary.

3. **Additive (add-k) smoothing is an anti-pattern here — decisive negative.**
   Smoothing over the full alphabet re-injects the rare big-leap tail the corpus
   almost never uses: big_leap 0.05 → 0.24, range 14 → 38, composite 0.81 → 0.57.
   The alphabet legally contains large leaps; in-context they're near-zero, and
   flooring them at add_k floods them back. Don't smooth this way. (Backoff, which
   only falls back to *observed* shorter-context counts, is the right regularizer.)

4. **The composite saturates across orders (0.79–0.82).** Three different-order
   models are statistically indistinguishable to the harness — first concrete
   evidence for backlog #9: first-order interval/contour/rep stats can't separate
   ngram1/2/3, so a higher-order self-similarity / monotony screen is what's
   needed to discriminate further (and to catch the whole-phrase-monotony gap run
   3 flagged). This bake-off is the natural home for that screen when it lands.

Outlier directive: uniform (structure-free floor) and add-k (the smoothing
rule-breaker) are the deliberate norm-breakers this cycle — and add-k's failure
is itself a useful result, not decoration.
