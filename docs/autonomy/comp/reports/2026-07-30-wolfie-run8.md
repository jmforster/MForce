## Front 1 — Contrast-aware fig B [metric] ✓ (backlog #2, top item)

fig B was sampled independent of fig A (`main()`: "contrast = TODO"). Now B is
drawn in relation to A and selected on a contrast objective:
`sample_contrast_figB` draws N=32 chain candidates (same note-count as A →
rhythmic-sibling bias) and keeps the best on **antecedent/consequent closure**
(0.45, small `|netA+netB|` — a rising A answered by a falling B, which also
controls range), **rhythmic kinship** (0.35, duration-profile overlap), and
**non-monotone B** (0.20, with a hard veto at zero-rate ≥ 0.5). The Markov joint
model stays faithful — B is a genuine draw, selection only.

Paired A/B driver `--contrast` (same figA + pattern/transform/placement per
pair; only B-sampling differs), n=14 pairs, both arms rendered + scored:

```
composite    independent=0.787  contrast=0.785  delta=-0.002
zero_rate    independent=0.278  contrast=0.251  delta=-0.027
range        independent=12.571 contrast=9.143  delta=-3.429
```

Read honestly: **range control is the real win** — contrast-B pulls the phrase
back into the corpus band (9.1 vs 12.6 semitones) and kills the runaways the
independent arm produced (p03 hit 24, p04 hit 29). zero_rate improves too.
Composite is a wash *by design*: the v1/v2 composite scores interval/contour
distribution + monotony, none of which measures whether B *balances* A. That
balance is the audition question — queued, not iterated. The closure objective
went through one measurable refinement (contour-complement → closure, then a
monotony veto) driven by observed range/zero defects; further composite-chasing
would be prohibited blind iteration and was not done.
Renders: `renders/markov_contrast/{independent,contrast}/` (14 pairs) + scores.csv.
Rerun: `python corpus/mtd_seg/markov_phrase.py --contrast --n 14`

## Front 2 — Neural next-note model [metric] ✓ (backlog #3, Matt G1)

`neural_nextnote.py`: a numpy-only Bengio-style neural LM over the same
(dstep,pulse) token stream — learned token embeddings → context window (C=3) →
tanh hidden (64) → softmax over the vocab, minibatch SGD with hand-written
backprop. This is the "LLM-like next-note prediction vs Markov" entrant from
GOALS. Soft-imported into `figuregen.build_methods` (numpy-free n-gram path
preserved), so `bake_off.py --tokens ...` trains + scores it automatically on
whatever corpus is loaded.

Training (MTD): val perplexity 56 → 28.5 over 12 epochs (~8 s), train_ce 3.30
vs val_ce 3.35 → learning, not overfitting. Bake-off, 80 melodies × 16 notes:

```
             MTD  comp_mean   Nottingham comp_mean   range(MTD/Nott)
corpus          0.869                    0.869        12.1 / 12.1
ngram2_backoff  0.876                    0.901        13.9 / 14.1
ngram3_backoff  0.864                    0.904        13.4 / 12.7
neural_mlp      0.855                    0.899        18.7 / 13.6
```

Finding: the neural is corpus-plausible and **competitive but not superior** —
on Nottingham it ties the best n-grams (0.899 vs 0.901/0.904); on MTD it trails
slightly with a wider range (18.7). Consistent with run-4's "composite saturates
across orders": a learned distributed representation doesn't out-plausible
count-based backoff on first-order interval stats at this melody scale / corpus
size. It now exists as a swappable method and is in the head-to-head, which is
the deliverable. (Its edge — generalization to unseen context via embedding
interpolation — would show at longer context / sparser data, not this metric.)
CSVs: `renders/bakeoff/{mtd,nottingham}_neural.csv`.
Rerun: `python corpus/mtd_seg/neural_nextnote.py --epochs 12` (standalone) or
`python corpus/mtd_seg/bake_off.py --k 80 [--tokens corpus/mtd_seg/nottingham_tokens.json]`.

## Front 4 — Figure transform library [build] ✓ (Matt's new REVIEW fragment)

`figure_transforms.py`: reusable transform family for non-literal repeats —
invert, retrograde, augment/diminish (rhythm), expand/compress_intervals (reach,
sign-preserving, never collapses a step to a repeat), rotate (cyclic), ornament
(passing/neighbor tone, total duration preserved), vary_tail (delegates to
markov_phrase). Every transform preserves the step0=0 anchor and never mutates
input; 90 invariant assertions pass. Plausibility check: original figures
composite 0.680, every transform within ±0.02 (0.662–0.700) → transforms add
variation without collapsing. A `TRANSFORMS` registry carries per-transform arity
for generic dispatch when wiring these into the phrase-builder (next step — see
backlog). This is the reusable substrate under Matt's spec-1/2/3 repeat variety.
Rerun: `python corpus/mtd_seg/figure_transforms.py`
