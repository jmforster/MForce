Overall verdict: much improvement, especially with removal of nonsense in bar 6.
Some of our "rules" "violated" (F-G over C vs. F-E), unless it was just some
unlucky dice rolls.
Some new "rules" surfaced:
1. B wants to to to C (can leap to D first, but leaping down from leading tone weird
2. Leaping to a non-chord tone (an appogiatura I guess) very unusual in this genre
3. Leaping to a non-chord tone *and then leaping to another non-chord tone* - ditto, x 2

The important point here is (as has been stated before) the rules we use are
dependent on the genre. All of my "objections" are in the context of nursery
rhyme songs.
