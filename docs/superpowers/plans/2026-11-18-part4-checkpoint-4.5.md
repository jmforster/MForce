# Part 4, checkpoint 4.5: the terminal types and the document's spellings — Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Part 4 section 8.1's fifth row, as decided with Matt on 2026-11-09: the last three node types the registry does not know become registered types, and the four spellings the codec will not read leave the files. `SoundChannel` and `StereoMixer` are registered parts in a new Output family, with declared pins, and the loader's plain-sound path reads those pins instead of reading the two nodes' JSON by hand; `PerformNode` is a registered type whose one enum setting, `field`, is the list the loader's branch reads against (75 → 78 registrations). A node reached from two of a plain sound's pull loops is refused by name. The editor's Channel and Mixer come from the registry, and it reads and writes `layout` and `comment`. `tools/convert_patch.py` gains five rules; 784 files in `patches/library`, `patches/baselines`, `patches/audition` and `patches/pending` are rewritten, 57 of them tracked: `inputs` becomes feeds in `params`; `ui` and `groups` become one `layout` block; `_comment` and `_provenance` become `comment`; an enum given as an index, or as a label in another case, becomes its declared label; two keys nothing reads are dropped. A file that still carries `inputs` fails to load in the engine and in the editor, naming the converter; the editor refuses the old layout and comment spellings the same way. No render in `patches/library` + `patches/baselines` moves. After this checkpoint every file in the four folders is in the form 4.6's codec reads.

**Architecture:** Part 4 sections 3.1, 3.4–3.7, 4.2–4.4, 4.8, 5.4, 5.8, 5.10, 6.7–6.9, 8.1–8.6 and 9.1 give the design; the survey behind this plan (2026-11-18: the loader whole, the three pin headers, the registry, the editor's thirty-four lines that name the two terminal types and its load, its two savers and its clipboards, the converter, the roundtrip gate and smoke, and a census of all 3,146 patch files — `renders/scratch/ab45/planning/census45.py`, `census45b.py`, `census45c.py`, reproduced in Task 0) adds the facts below, each of which a gate or a test then proves.
